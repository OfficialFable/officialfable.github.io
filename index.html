FAQ

Good questions.

Is Fable really free?

Yes. Fable is free to download and use, and it has no adverts.

Why are songs 30 seconds long?

Right now Fable plays official 30-second previews, which apps are allowed to stream for free. Full-length songs need a licensing partner, and that’s on our roadmap. Everything you do in Fable today (rooms, tournaments, friends) will work the same with full songs.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that safe?

That message appears for new apps that haven’t bought a code-signing certificate yet. Click More info, then Run anyway to install Fable.

Do I need an account?

You can look around offline with demo friends, but you’ll need a free account to add real friends, join rooms and appear on leaderboards.

Is Fable on Mac, iPhone or Android?

Not yet. Fable is Windows-only for now. Other platforms may come later.

Where do I get help or report a bug?

Join the Fable Discord and post in #help or #bug-reports. You can also download Fable and find rooms there.

How many people can join a room?

Up to 50. The host can make people admins, and the room can vote someone in as admin too.
