The rules, in plain English.

The short version

Community rules

These apply to everything you put in Fable: your name, username, profile picture, room names, chat and reactions.

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Keep it safe. No sexual, violent, gory or illegal content.
  • Keep it private. Never share someone’s personal information, including your own.
  • No spam or advertising. No flooding chat, scams, or promoting other apps, servers or products.
  • Be yourself. Don’t pretend to be someone else, including the Fable Team.
  • Play fair. No cheating in tournaments, and no bots or tools that fake plays or scores.

Reports and moderation

If someone breaks these rules, use ⋮ → Report in the app. Reports are private and reviewed by the Fable Team. To keep Fable safe, we may remove content, reset a name or picture, take away tournament results, or suspend or close an account. We usually warn first, but serious or repeated problems can lead straight to a ban.
