Privacy policy

Your data, in plain English.

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version
  • Fable has no adverts, sells nothing about you, and uses no tracking or analytics.
  • We store what’s needed for accounts, friends, rooms and leaderboards, and nothing else.
  • Your listening activity is only visible to your friends.
  • You can delete your account, and everything linked to it, from inside the app.

Who we are

Fable is a free desktop music app for listening with friends. In this policy “Fable”, “we” and “us” mean the people who make Fable. You need to be at least 13 years old to create a Fable account.

What we store when you create an account

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send you password-reset codes.
  • Your password, stored securely (hashed) by our account provider. We can never see it.
  • Your username, display name and colour, and your profile picture if you add one.

What we store while you use Fable

  • Friends: who you’re friends with, friend requests, and people you’ve blocked.
  • What you’re listening to: the current song and artist, whether it’s playing, and when it last changed, so friends can see it.
  • Tournaments: how many times you played each artist this week, and your best Guess the Song score each week.
  • Listening rooms: rooms you’re in, your role (host, admin, member), when you were last active there, admin votes, songs you add, and chat messages and reactions you send.
  • Reports: if you report someone, or someone reports you, the report and a snapshot of the reported person’s name, picture and recent room messages, so we can review it.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, liked songs, play queue and personal listening stats are saved on your own computer, not on our servers. If you use Fable offline, nothing about you is sent to us at all.

Who can see what

  • Anyone signed in to Fable can find your username, display name, colour and profile picture (so friends can add you).
  • Only your friends can see what you’re listening to, your play counts and your scores.
  • People in the same room can see your name, picture, role and room chat.
  • Blocked people can’t find you, add you, or send you friend requests.

Services we use

  • Supabase runs Fable’s accounts, database and picture storage.
  • Deezer provides song information and 30-second previews. The app requests these directly from Deezer, so Deezer receives your IP address the same way any website you visit does. Their own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the Fable download.

We don’t use advertising networks, analytics tools or tracking pixels, and we never sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. Weekly play counts and scores are kept so past weeks still make sense. Reports are kept while we review them and afterwards for safety.

Deleting your account

In Fable, open Settings → Privacy → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, picture, friends, plays, scores and room memberships. Chat messages you sent in rooms stay so conversations still make sense, but your name is removed from them.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in Settings → Profile, and delete everything with Delete account. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights over your data, such as asking for a copy of it. To do that, or to ask anything about this policy, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For big changes, we’ll also let you know in Fable.
